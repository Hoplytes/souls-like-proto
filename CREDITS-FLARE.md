# Credits: Flare art

The hero's equipment pieces (character creator), the tiles of every biome
(prairie, ruins, snow, caves, dungeon), the enemies (skeletons, zombies,
goblins, antlions, ants, the Minotaur and the wyvern), the music and the
sound effects (ambiences, footsteps, enemy sounds, teleporter) come from **Flare** (Free Libre Action Roleplaying Engine),
https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game — licensed **CC-BY-SA 3.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

`tools/convert_flare.py` changed them: scaled down, cut out of their atlases
and re-packed into one sheet per animation; the music was re-encoded at a
lower bit rate. These modified files, in `assets/flare/` and
`assets/flare_audio/`, are shared under the same CC-BY-SA 3.0 licence.
